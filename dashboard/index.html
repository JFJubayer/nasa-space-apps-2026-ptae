    Method and evidence

    What PTAE measures, how the three dimensions are combined, how far to trust the result, and what Earth can never reproduce.

    How a score is built

    TerrainMeasured

    Slope is measured over identical ground baselines (240 m to 3.84 km) on a common 120 m grid, so a 20 m lunar DEM, a 463 m Mars DEM and a 30 m Earth DEM become comparable. Every Earth region is scanned with sliding 40 km windows and the best-matching window is reported.

    EnvironmentMeasured

    NASA POWER climate (temperature, daily range, rain, humidity, pressure) compared with a target clipped to the most extreme values reachable on Earth. This measures “as close as Earth gets”, never equality.

    GeologyCurated judgement

    Expert tags such as impact structures, basaltic volcanism, lava tubes, ground ice and evaporites. These are priors, not measurements; they are weighted 25% by default and you can change that.

    CombineGeometric mean

    A weighted geometric mean means a site must be adequate on every dimension. Strong slope statistics cannot rescue a wet, vegetated valley. The additive mean is available under “Adjust weights”.
